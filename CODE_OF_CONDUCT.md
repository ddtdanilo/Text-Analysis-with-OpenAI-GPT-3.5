# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and technically honest.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never request real sensitive documents for debugging.
- Give credit and link authoritative sources.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Accept corrections gracefully and resolve disagreement with evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained disruption are not
tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept
confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers may remove content, close
threads, or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
